What to include

  • Bug reports: the page you were on (country, category, Trending or Most Viewed), your browser, and what you expected to happen.
  • Content corrections: the video URL and what is wrong with the listing.
  • Removal requests: the video URL and the right you hold. Note that TrendVid only embeds public YouTube videos — removing the video from YouTube removes it here too, and that remains the fastest route.
  • Advertising & partnerships: tell us your product category, target countries and budget, and we will send available placements.
  • Affiliate & API enquiries: mention “API” if you would like programmatic access to the trending feed.

Abuse and safety

If you believe a listed video breaks YouTube’s own policies, please report it to YouTube first — that removes it from the source data and therefore from TrendVid as well.
